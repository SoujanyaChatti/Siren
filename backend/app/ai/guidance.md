# SIREN approved flood-safety guidance

Short snippets paraphrased from National Weather Service (NWS) "Turn Around Don't Drown" and flood safety
pages, and Ready.gov flood guidance. Ask SIREN may only answer from these snippets plus current incident
facts. Each line: `ID | English | Spanish | source`.

G01 | Turn around, don't drown. Never drive or walk into floodwater. | Dé la vuelta, no se ahogue. Nunca maneje ni camine en agua de inundación. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown
G02 | Just 12 inches of moving water can carry away most cars. | Solo 12 pulgadas de agua en movimiento pueden arrastrar la mayoría de los carros. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown
G03 | Two feet of rushing water can carry away most vehicles, including SUVs and pickups. | Dos pies de agua rápida pueden arrastrar la mayoría de los vehículos, incluso camionetas. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown
G04 | Six inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult off their feet. | Seis pulgadas de agua rápida pueden tumbar a un adulto. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown
G05 | Do not drive through flooded underpasses or low-water crossings; water is often deeper than it looks. | No maneje por pasos a desnivel o cruces bajos inundados; el agua suele ser más profunda de lo que parece. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during
G06 | If water rises around your car, leave it and move to higher ground if it is safe to do so. | Si el agua sube alrededor de su carro, déjelo y vaya a un lugar más alto si es seguro hacerlo. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during
G07 | If you are told to evacuate, do so immediately and follow routes given by local officials. | Si le piden evacuar, hágalo de inmediato y siga las rutas indicadas por las autoridades locales. | https://www.ready.gov/floods
G08 | Move to higher ground or a higher floor if flooding threatens your home. Avoid closed attics where you could be trapped. | Suba a un lugar o piso más alto si la inundación amenaza su casa. Evite áticos cerrados donde podría quedar atrapado. | https://www.ready.gov/floods
G09 | If you are trapped in a building by floodwater, go to the highest level and call 911. | Si queda atrapado por el agua en un edificio, suba al nivel más alto y llame al 911. | https://www.ready.gov/floods
G10 | Stay off bridges over fast-moving water. | No use puentes sobre agua que corre rápido. | https://www.ready.gov/floods
G11 | Floodwater can hide debris, open drains and downed power lines, and can be contaminated. | El agua de inundación puede esconder escombros, alcantarillas abiertas y cables eléctricos caídos, y puede estar contaminada. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during
G12 | Avoid walking or driving at night in flooded areas, when it is harder to see danger. | Evite caminar o manejar de noche en zonas inundadas, cuando es más difícil ver el peligro. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown
G13 | Help people who need extra time to move, such as older adults and people with disabilities, make a plan early. | Ayude a quienes necesitan más tiempo para moverse, como personas mayores o con discapacidad, a planear con tiempo. | https://www.ready.gov/disability
G14 | Keep medicines, documents, a phone charger, water and food ready in a go-bag. | Tenga listos medicinas, documentos, cargador de teléfono, agua y comida en una mochila de emergencia. | https://www.ready.gov/kit
G15 | Listen to local officials and NWS for warnings and instructions; SIREN is not an official warning system. | Escuche a las autoridades locales y al NWS para advertencias e instrucciones; SIREN no es un sistema oficial. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood
G16 | A Flash Flood Warning means flooding is happening or about to happen: act now and move to higher ground. | Una Advertencia de Inundación Repentina significa que la inundación ocurre o está por ocurrir: actúe ya y suba a un lugar alto. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-watch-warning
G17 | A Flood Watch means flooding is possible: be ready to act. | Una Vigilancia de Inundación significa que es posible una inundación: esté listo para actuar. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-watch-warning
G18 | Do not let children play in or near floodwater or drainage ditches. | No deje que los niños jueguen en o cerca del agua de inundación o de zanjas. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during
G19 | If your car stalls in water, do not try to restart it in rising water; get out and move to higher ground. | Si su carro se apaga en el agua, no intente encenderlo con el agua subiendo; salga y vaya a un lugar alto. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-during
G20 | After flooding, return home only when officials say it is safe. | Después de la inundación, regrese a casa solo cuando las autoridades digan que es seguro. | https://www.ready.gov/floods
